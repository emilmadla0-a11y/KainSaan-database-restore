About KainSaan

KainSaan helps you decide where to eat in and around Angeles City, Pampanga. Each restaurant has its cuisine, address, rating, and contact number in one place, so you can compare options without opening five tabs.

How to use it

  1. Open Restaurants to see the full list, or search for a restaurant by name.
  2. Filter by cuisine, or sort by rating or name.
  3. Select a restaurant to see its address and contact number.
  4. Can't decide? Select Surprise me and KainSaan picks one for you.

Where the information comes from

Restaurant records are managed by the KainSaan team and stored in a MongoDB database. Ratings are the figures saved with each record, so they may differ from the latest ratings on other sites.

About the name

KainSaan joins two Filipino words: kain (to eat) and saan (where). The name is also a playful nod to the universal reply when asked where to eat: 'Kahit saan.'
